Launch kit · strategy, films & social

Quolio

Be in the room. We'll take the notes.

An iPhone, iPad and Mac notebook that turns the meeting you just sat through into a note with a summary, decisions and to-dos. It opens instantly and works without a signal. This kit holds the launch plan and three films, drawn frame by frame in code in a torn-paper storybook style.

0:00 / 0:50

The films play here without sound. Every film is exported twice to marketing/out/: 16:9 at 1920×1080 for X, YouTube and LinkedIn, and 9:16 at 1080×1920 for Reels, TikTok and Shorts (quolio-*-vertical.mp4). The vertical cuts use a moving camera, a stacked layout for "Two seconds", and captions kept inside the apps' safe zones. Both versions include an original soundtrack, also made in code (audio.mjs): a music-box theme in F major with pencil, paper, train and chime sound effects, mastered to −16 LUFS for social. It's all original, so there's nothing to license.

Positioning

One sentence for everyone, including us.

For people who spend their day in meetings, Quolio is the notebook that listens for you. It turns what was said into a clean note with decisions and owners, so you can look people in the eye instead of at your keyboard. Unlike cloud notes apps and meeting bots, it opens in a blink, works offline and never joins your call as a stranger.

Launch plan

Timed from launch day (L). It's tied to the build: TestFlight build 3 is out, and the public launch waits until on-device transcription ships.

TeaseL−6 to L−4 weeks
  • Post the hero film on X with a build-in-public thread: "Every frame of this was drawn in code. Here's the app it's about."
  • Put up a one-page waitlist site with the film looping at the top and one email field.
  • Seed short clips twice a week: the scribbling triptych, the strips flying into the phone, the night train.
Private betaL−4 to L−1 weeks
  • Invite waitlist users to TestFlight in waves of 100, PMs and founders first.
  • Ask one question after their first recorded meeting: "Would you be upset if this went away?" Collect quotes for launch.
  • Recruit 20 beta users to post their own "pencil down" story on launch day.
Launch weekL to L+7 days
  • Launch on Product Hunt on Tuesday, with the hero film as the gallery video and "Two seconds" as the first comment.
  • Post "Pencil down" on LinkedIn and "Two seconds" on X, Reels and Shorts.
  • Post a Show HN write-up about how the films were drawn in JavaScript. The craft story travels on its own.
  • Offer Pro free for 30 days to anyone who records a meeting in week one.
Keep goingL+2 to L+8 weeks
  • Weekly "Meeting of the week": one real, anonymized note from a user, shown as a 10-second paper animation.
  • Publish a Notion-import guide and a "Notion vs. Quolio: cold open" screen recording.
  • Apple Search Ads on "meeting notes", "notes offline" and "transcribe meeting", capped at $20 a day until conversion is known.

X · Build-in-public thread
1/ We made our launch film without a camera, a studio or After Effects. Every frame is drawn in JavaScript. 🧵

2/ The style is torn paper. Each shape is a polygon with a jagged, hand-torn edge, a pale fibre outline and a grain texture.

3/ The stop-motion wobble is a cheat: the torn edges get re-cut 6 times a second, cycling between 3 variants.

4/ The whole film is one function, render(t). Headless Chrome calls it frame by frame and pipes the frames to ffmpeg. The music is synthesized in code too.

5/ The app it's about is Quolio: be in the room, and it takes the notes. Waitlist → [waitlist link]

Before anything goes public

The films and copy make product promises. Each one has to be true on launch day.

  • Meeting → note depends on transcription. The README still lists whisper.cpp as a stub, so ship it before the hero film goes out as a launch ad.
  • "Under 2 seconds" is a test target. Measure a cold launch on the iPhone 12 Pro and put the real number in the copy.
  • "No bot on the call" / offline: the recording and notes are local. If summaries go to Gemini, say "works offline, summaries sync when you're back online" rather than implying everything happens on the device.
  • iPad & Mac: the Mac Catalyst target exists. Confirm it's in the launch build, or drop "Mac" from the end card.
  • Recording consent: the app, the App Store text and the films should all nudge people to tell others in the room they're recording.
