## X: Build-in-public thread
Images: `x-1-be-in-the-room.png`

```
1/ We made our launch film without a camera, a studio or After Effects. Every frame is drawn in JavaScript. 🧵

2/ The style is torn paper. Each shape is a polygon with a jagged, hand-torn edge, a pale fibre outline and a grain texture.

3/ The stop-motion wobble is a cheat: the torn edges get re-cut 6 times a second, cycling between 3 variants.

4/ The whole film is one function, render(t). Headless Chrome calls it frame by frame and pipes the frames to ffmpeg. The music is synthesized in code too.

5/ The app it's about is Quolio: be in the room, and it takes the notes. Waitlist → [waitlist link]
```

## LinkedIn: Founder post
Images: `li-1-stop-choosing.png`

```
I used to leave every meeting with two kinds of notes: the ones I wrote, and the ones I wish I had written.

The problem wasn't effort. You can't really listen and take notes at the same time, so one of them always loses.

So we're building Quolio. Put your phone on the table, hit record, and actually be in the conversation. Afterwards you get a note with:
→ a short summary
→ the decisions that were made
→ the to-dos, with owners

No bot joins your call. It just listens in the room, right from your phone.

We're opening a small beta for people who spend most of their week in meetings. If that's you, the waitlist is here: [waitlist link]

What's the one thing you always lose from a meeting?
```
