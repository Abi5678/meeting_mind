Meetings in. Notes out.

Quolio records your meetings, lectures and talks and turns them into notes you can use: a summary, action items and a searchable transcript. The AI runs on your device. No account.

What it does

  • Summaries, decisions and action items from a recording.
  • Lectures and talks written up as key points and takeaways.
  • Import audio or video files, and write or draw in your notes.
  • Study with quizzes and flashcards made from your notes.

AI features need Apple Intelligence: iOS 26 on iPhone 15 Pro or newer, or a Mac with Apple silicon (M-series).
